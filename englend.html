Bukayo Saka & Jude Bellingham

Bukayo Saka (Arsenal) and Jude Bellingham (Real Madrid) represent the heart of England. Both stars will be right at the absolute peak prime of their careers for the 2030 tournament, making England a scary team to face.

Quick 2030 Stats

  • Home Country: England
  • Saka's Position: Right Winger
  • Bellingham's Position: Attacking Midfielder
  • Main Skills: Breathtaking ball control, immense physical strength, and scoring clutch goals.

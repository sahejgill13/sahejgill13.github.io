Harry Kane & Jude Bellingham

Harry Kane (Bayern Munich) and Jude Bellingham (Real Madrid) represent the heart of England. Both stars will be right at the absolute peak prime of their careers for the 2030 tournament, making England a hard team to face.

Quick 2030 Stats

Kylian Mbappé

Kylian Mbappé is a legendary forward from France. He already won a World Cup when he was just a teenager. By the time the 2030 World Cup arrives, he will be the ultimate veteran captain aiming to bring the trophy back to France one last time!

Quick 2030 Stats

  • Home Country: France
  • Field Position: Forward (Striker)
  • Mbappé's Age in 2030: He will be 31 years old.
  • Main Skill: Incredible leadership, big-game experience, and lethal finishing.
